Half-adder sequence

1. XOR routes to Sum. 2. AND routes to Carry. 3. The return circuit brings the same marble back to repeat. Both modules share the same preset A/B inputs.

The two outputs are sampled sequentially; a complete lap publishes the pair. This is a routing-based half-adder, not a new conditional opcode in the seven-instruction INC/JUMP machine.
